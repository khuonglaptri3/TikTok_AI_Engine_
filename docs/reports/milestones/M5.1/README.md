**`M5.1` is `COMPLETE` (`Gate G5` / `M5.G` remains `NOT_RUN` pending `M5.2` completion): observable comment indicator detection, `K` adversarial peer-review & TDD evaluator hardening (`10/10` M5.1 unit tests), blinded two-contributor annotation across `300` real fresh `M3.3` comments (`22` videos from `m3_3_pilot_snapshot_v2`: `N = 180/180` verified + `K = 180/180` verified, `60` double-annotated), joint adjudication, video-disjoint held-out `1/3` evaluation, and `22`-video real-corpus ABSA filter sensitivity are complete and verified. Automated comment filtering remains disabled (`recommended_mode = "diagnostic_only_without_filtering"`, `hide_seeding = False` by default).**

- **Blinded Two-Person Real Annotation & Agreement (`300` comments / `22` videos):** Both `N` (`180/180`) and `K` (`180/180`) annotated [m5_1_pilot_pool.jsonl](../../../../data/annotations/m5_1_pilot_pool.jsonl) strictly blinded to [m5_1_pilot_pool_locked_predictions.jsonl](../../../../data/annotations/m5_1_pilot_pool_locked_predictions.jsonl) (and `K` blinded to `N_label` during overlap labeling). On the `60` `double_review_N_K` overlap comments, raw 6-class agreement is **`56/60 = 93.33%`** (`cohens_kappa = 0.4760` under `90.3%` majority-class prevalence) and **binary positive-indicator agreement is `60/60 = 100.0%` (`positive_indicator_cohens_kappa = 1.0000`)**; all `4` disagreements (`m5_1_005..007` creator bare `@mentions` and `m5_1_031` colloquial TikTok idiom `"Tới công chuyện với tui"`) lay strictly on the `no_indicator_observed <-> unknown_ambiguous` boundary and were adjudicated to `unknown_ambiguous` per [annotation_guidelines.md](evidence/annotation_guidelines.md).
- **Adjudicated Real Class Distribution & Organic-Duplicate Fix (`300` comments):** `no_indicator_observed: 271` (`90.33%`), `noise_or_empty: 14` (`4.67%`), `unknown_ambiguous: 13` (`4.33%`), `contact_or_link: 1` (`0.33%`), `template_phrase_match: 1` (`0.33%`), `observable_duplicate: 0` (`0.00%`). Guarding duplicate clustering with `min_duplicate_words = 5` + 5–8 syllable Vietnamese diner inquiry exemptions reduced real-pool organic-duplicate false positives from **`2 -> 0`** (and **`24 -> 0`** on the synthetic edge fixture).
- **Video-Disjoint Held-Out Evaluation & Fail-Closed Diagnostic Policy ([m5_1_real_pilot_eval_v1.json](../../../../artifacts/benchmarks/m5_1_real_pilot_eval_v1.json)):** Partitioning the `22` videos strictly by `video_id` (`seed=42`, `0` video overlap across `dev=8` videos / `126` rows, `calibration=7` videos / `128` rows, `held_out_third=7` videos / `46` rows) yielded `noise_or_empty Precision = 1.0000`, `Recall = 1.0000`, `F1 = 1.0000` (`6/6` TP, active-class `95% CI [0.0000, 1.0000]`) on `held_out_third`. Because `contact_or_link` and `observable_duplicate` had `0` positive support on `held_out_third` (`core_positive_classes_missing_support = ["contact_or_link", "observable_duplicate"]`) and full-pool (`300` rows) macro precision/recall across observed positive classes was `0.3095` (`noise_or_empty F1 = 0.9286`, while coded TikTok slang `"sàn Cam ... kikitea"` in `m5_1_292`, serialized `[大笑]` stickers in `m5_1_011`, and multi-bullet promo copy in `m5_1_165` went unflagged), `auto_filter_eligible` evaluates to **`False`** and `/api/v1/trust/audit-video` + `/demo/` retain **`diagnostic_only_without_filtering`** (`comment_filter_policy = "diagnostic_only_unvalidated"`, `hide_seeding = False`).
- **Real-Corpus ABSA Filter Sensitivity (`22` pilot videos):** Across `22` real `M3.3` videos (`15` flagged emoji/noise comments across `10` videos, `0.6818` avg flagged/video), filtering observable noise comments caused `0.0000` shift in `viewer_mean` and `supported_sentiment_discrepancy` (`max_abs_mean_score_shift = 0.0000`, whereas duplicate-contaminated fixture threads shifted by up to `0.5000`), confirming zero distortion when diagnostic badges are surfaced alongside unhidden comments.
